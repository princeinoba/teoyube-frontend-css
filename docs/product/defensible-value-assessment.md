# Defensible value assessment

## Thesis

Teoyube's defensible value is not "an AI that knows God's will." It is a governed formation system that keeps Scripture authoritative while making recommendations inspectable, cautious, consent-aware, cross-module, and reversible.

| Claimed component | Status | Evidence | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Exact Scripture-grounded retrieval | **DEMONSTRATED locally** | Exact WEB corpus; 356/356 references; citation validity 1.0 | One translation; no real-user/pastoral comprehension evidence |
| Structured spiritual journeys | **DEMONSTRATED in automated local flow** | Ten typed artifacts; end-to-end continuity; skip/edit/reject/undo | No participant completion evidence |
| Explainable recommendations | **DEMONSTRATED locally** | TIG source path, graph traversal, breakdown, confidence, limitations, fallback | Explanation usefulness not user-tested |
| Humble AI language | **DEMONSTRATED in locked/synthetic evidence** | Safety rules, Gate A, Prompt 19K strict outputs | Limited locked set; cultural/pastoral fit unknown |
| User-owned memory | **PARTIALLY DEMONSTRATED** | Consent, encryption, export/delete, revocation, isolation | Production identity/KMS/storage/operations absent |
| Cross-module continuity | **DEMONSTRATED in automated local flow** | Today through Tomorrow; retrieval adapters; provenance preserved | Longitudinal real use not observed |
| Discernment rather than certainty | **DEMONSTRATED in contracts, not user-validated** | Prohibited certainty, suggestive calling language, user-confirmed writes | Users may still misread authority; study needed |
| Reflection and testimony continuity | **PARTIALLY DEMONSTRATED** | User review, no automatic fulfillment/divine claim, reversible Book promotion | No sustained-use or testimony-authority research |

Overall status: **ARCHITECTURALLY_IMPLEMENTED_BUT_NOT_VALIDATED**.

## Why this could be defensible

- The doctrine is encoded in contracts rather than left solely to prompts.
- Deterministic Scripture, TIG, consent, and safety layers constrain model behavior.
- The original visual identity and owner-approved evolution are protected by executable evidence.
- Provenance travels through modules instead of disappearing at each feature boundary.
- User agency is explicit: edit, reject, skip, undo, export, delete, and confirm.

## Why it is not yet a market or ministry outcome claim

- No real-user research or stabilization evidence exists.
- No production operation, public deployment, or longitudinal continuity is claimed.
- No pastoral review result has been recorded for the current product.
- Accessibility and dependency blockers remain.
- Production infrastructure and unit economics are not validated.

The next defensibility step is evidence, not more surface area: stabilize, resolve current release/security debt, run an ethical pilot, prove production controls, then re-score.
