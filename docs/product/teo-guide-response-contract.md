# Teo Guide response contract

Every structured Teo Guide response distinguishes:

- acknowledgement;
- exact Scripture;
- textual context;
- Teoyube interpretation;
- Promise connections;
- calling evidence;
- prayer draft;
- practical actions;
- reflection prompts;
- testimony/Book candidates;
- mentor or community discussion;
- why the response was selected;
- confidence;
- limitations;
- source records;
- action proposals;
- public safety metadata;
- dataset, ruleset, corpus, planner, registry, orchestrator, and policy versions.

Every response has at least one source with an authority label, path, and version. Scripture text is exact approved WEB content and is labeled Scripture. Context remains textual context. Teoyube interpretation, TIG evidence, Promise Clusters, prayer language, and actions are labeled or worded as aids, not Scripture or divine speech.

Calling language uses emerging indicators and limitations; it never states a final destiny. Prayer drafts are editable and do not command God or guarantee an outcome. Major decisions encourage prayer, Scripture in context, wise counsel, community, and appropriate qualified care. Only the user may finalize testimony, identify fulfillment, or promote a reviewed record to the Book.

Critical safety responses place immediate safety and resource guidance before Scripture or spiritual recommendations. Sensitive responses preserve uncertainty and appropriate human/professional escalation.

The client-safe DTO contains only display-safe sections, source references, proposal summaries, and public safety state. Server plans, tool inputs, raw memory, seeds, traversal, caches, secrets, and internal authorization details are excluded.
