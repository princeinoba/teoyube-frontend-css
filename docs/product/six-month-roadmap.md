# Six-month evidence-led roadmap

The roadmap improves the current product behind the approved Teoyube interface. It does not authorize redesign, baseline replacement, public deployment, paid calls, production enablement, or Prompt 23B deletion.

## Month 1 — stabilization, security, and accessibility

**Outcomes**

- Regenerate a current, source-bound Gate C evidence bundle under separate authorization.
- Reassess GHSA-mh99-v99m-4gvg and upstream ESLint/minimatch/brace-expansion compatibility; keep the existing blocker if no safe fix exists.
- Re-run a current non-destructive inventory and dependency graph.
- Reproduce and prioritize focus, aria-hidden, contrast, zoom, keyboard, and responsive accessibility debt.
- Begin a dated incident/stabilization log.

**Exit evidence**

- Recovery/runtime/contracts remain PASS; protected baseline changes zero unless narrowly owner-approved.
- Gate C lineage has zero stale-artifact failures.
- Full dependency audit is zero high/critical, or a separate current owner exception is explicitly documented.
- Accessibility issues have severity, route, reproduction, owner scope, test, and rollback.

## Month 2 — real-user pilot

**Outcomes**

- Recruit 12–20 participants across intended experience levels plus a pastoral/theological review group.
- Test Search, Today loop, Scripture/source inspection, calling language, prayer, reflection, testimony consent, export/delete, and recovery from rejection/undo.
- Collect consented bounded aggregates and de-identified thematic notes; never collect raw prayer/reflection by default.

**Exit evidence**

- Research status moves from `USER_RESEARCH_NOT_YET_RUN` to a reviewed result.
- At least 80% correctly distinguish Scripture from interpretation/prayer/action.
- At least 90% complete core safety/reversibility tasks without severe confusion.
- Every severe safety, coercion, privacy, or accessibility issue is blocked or resolved.

## Month 3 — production infrastructure

**Outcomes**

- Implement production adapters for identity/session, relational storage, KMS/key rotation, consent, partitioned vector storage, distributed rate limiting, backup/restore, and privacy-safe observability.
- Preserve domain and UI contracts; keep live AI/vector flags off.

**Exit evidence**

- Cross-tenant isolation, revocation, deletion, CSRF, rotation, restore, and operator-access tests pass in a production-shaped environment.
- No raw private spiritual text appears in logs, metrics, traces, caches, or backups.
- Recovery and owner-approved visual evidence remains unchanged.

## Month 4 — production AI and retrieval qualification

**Outcomes**

- Expand locked AI/retrieval evaluations with pilot-derived, de-identified cases.
- Improve source precision without reducing citation validity, recall, consent filtering, or safety.
- Requalify provider/model versions and production budget controls through an explicitly authorized paid-evaluation gate.

**Exit evidence**

- Exact citation validity 1.0; fabricated citations 0; critical safety recall 1.0; injection bypass 0.
- Recall@10 at least 0.95 and source precision at least 0.50 on the expanded set.
- Gate B Production criteria are satisfied but production remains disabled until owner cutover.

## Month 5 — controlled deployment

**Outcomes**

- Run an owner-controlled, reversible limited pilot deployment.
- Exercise rollback, backup restore, incident escalation, spend caps, kill switches, and support workflows.
- Monitor only consented, content-free product and operational metrics.

**Exit evidence**

- Current Gate C PASS at deployed commit; rollback drill passes; no unresolved critical/high security or safety incident.
- Performance and cost remain within budgets for seven days.
- Production deployment scope and user population are explicitly owner-approved.

## Month 6 — pilot analysis and selective cleanup

**Outcomes**

- Analyze pilot comprehension, faithful-action selection, reflection continuity, trust, reversibility, safety, accessibility, incidents, performance, and cost.
- Re-score all ten dimensions from evidence.
- Re-run Prompt 23 inventory; seek candidate-level owner decisions; execute only separately authorized cleanup after parity and rollback proofs.

**Exit evidence**

- A real-user outcome report, production operations report, updated scorecard, and current risk register exist.
- Stabilization is documented.
- Prompt 23B remains locked unless every prerequisite and candidate-level decision is satisfied.
