## Consent language

Participation is voluntary. The participant may pause, skip, or withdraw
without explanation. Spiritual reflections need not be disclosed. Sessions
should avoid collecting prayer, journal, testimony, trauma, health,
relationship, account credentials, or identifying details. Recording requires
separate explicit consent. Participation does not provide pastoral, medical,
legal, or crisis care.

## Participant tasks

1. Complete the daily loop and use skip, revisit, reject, and undo.
2. Find a Scripture and verify the displayed WEB source.
3. Inspect why a recommendation was selected and identify its limitations.
4. Reject and undo a recommendation.
5. Inspect and delete consented memory.
6. Distinguish Scripture, interpretation, application, prayer, and suggested
   action.
7. Use deterministic Teo Guide.
8. With separate external-AI consent in protected preview, use live AI.
9. Respond to a simulated provider fallback.
10. Explain the calling and testimony limitations.
11. Complete keyboard-only and 200% zoom tasks on desktop and mobile layouts.

## Interview guide

- What did you believe was Scripture versus Teoyube interpretation?
- Why did Teoyube make its recommendation?
- What felt uncertain or limited?
- Did reject, edit, skip, undo, consent, and delete feel understandable?
- Did any language feel coercive, spiritually authoritative, unsafe, or
  shaming?
- Did cross-module context move only when expected?
- What would you want a trusted person, pastor, or professional to review?
- Which keyboard, focus, zoom, or screen-reader step was difficult?

## Safety and escalation

The facilitator stops the task for immediate-danger disclosure, unsafe
guidance, privacy exposure, fabricated citation, distress, or participant
request. The product is not used for crisis intervention. Follow the
appropriate reviewed local support protocol and preserve no raw disclosure in
the research notes.

## Anonymization and analysis

Assign a random participant code. Store consent separately. Record task
outcomes and categorized observations, not raw spiritual content. Redact names,
locations, exact quotations, and account identifiers. Limit access, define a
deletion date before collection, and honor withdrawal/deletion.
