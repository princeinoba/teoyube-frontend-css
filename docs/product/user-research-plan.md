# Teoyube real-user research plan

Status: **USER_RESEARCH_NOT_YET_RUN**.

## Research questions

1. Can users distinguish exact Scripture, textual context, Teoyube interpretation, personalized application, prayer draft, suggested action, and uncertainty?
2. Can users move through Today → Check-in → Scripture → Promise → Prayer → Calling discernment → Assignment → Reflection → Testimony candidate → Book review → Tomorrow without losing provenance?
3. Do users understand that calling is discernment over time, not a divine verdict?
4. Can users inspect why a recommendation appeared, reject/edit/skip it, undo transitions, and export/delete approved memory?
5. Does the experience feel spiritually useful, humble, non-coercive, and emotionally safe?
6. Can keyboard, screen-reader, zoom, touch, and reduced-motion users complete the same tasks?

## Participants

- 12–20 primary participants across new, returning, and experienced Bible-study users.
- Include intended desktop/mobile use, varied accessibility needs, and varied familiarity with AI.
- A separate 4–6 member pastoral/theological review panel with more than one church/cultural perspective.
- Exclude minors and actively crisis-presenting individuals from the initial product study; provide appropriate support routing rather than research participation.

## Sessions

1. **Comprehension baseline:** identify the authority/type of six labeled content blocks.
2. **Search and source inspection:** find a promise from a life situation and verify exact Scripture/context.
3. **Daily loop:** complete or intentionally skip/revisit each moment and inspect preserved context.
4. **Calling discernment:** interpret confidence, limitations, graph/source path, and suggestive wording.
5. **Prayer/reflection/testimony:** edit a draft, decline storage, create a candidate, reject publication, and undo.
6. **Memory ownership:** grant/revoke purposes, export records, delete one record and the account.
7. **Failure/safety probes:** misleading prompt, divine-certainty wording, unsupported citation, sensitive topic, provider fallback.
8. **Accessibility:** keyboard-only, screen reader, 200% zoom, narrow viewport, reduced motion, and touch where applicable.

## Measures

- Authority distinction accuracy: target at least 80% per participant and 90% aggregate after onboarding.
- Core task completion without facilitator rescue: at least 90%.
- Source/explanation discovery: at least 90%.
- Reject/edit/undo/export/delete completion: 100% for safety-critical tasks.
- Median clarity, trust, and perceived humility: at least 4/5.
- Severe coercion, fabricated Scripture, hidden write, cross-user exposure, or crisis-routing failure: zero tolerance.
- Qualitative themes for spiritual usefulness, discomfort, cultural fit, and pastoral acceptability; do not create a spiritual score.

## Privacy and ethics

- Obtain research and purpose-specific analytics consent separately.
- Use synthetic or participant-authored disposable text; raw prayers, reflections, testimony, health details, or crisis disclosures are not copied into analytics or reports.
- Allow skip, withdrawal, note deletion, and data export.
- De-identify notes; store only bounded task outcomes and reviewed themes.
- Do not infer faithfulness, maturity, calling, mental state, or divine action.
- Pause immediately for distress and provide human/pastoral/professional support guidance appropriate to the situation.

## Analysis and decision rules

- Two reviewers independently code severe safety, authority confusion, coercion, privacy, and accessibility findings.
- Any severe finding blocks expansion until fixed and retested.
- Segment results only when consented and sufficiently aggregated; no hidden profiling.
- Owner reviews visible changes through the established approval protocol.
- Scores for Product Vision, UX, and Spiritual Experience remain below 9 until this plan produces reviewed evidence.
