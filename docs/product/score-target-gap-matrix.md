## Cross-cutting exit criteria

No dimension may be promoted to 9.0 merely because another dimension improved. A 9.0 overall claim requires all of the following at once: current Gate C Preview PASS, production-readiness evidence appropriate to the claimed deployment, real-user research completed, no concealed high/critical dependency blocker, current visual/accessibility evidence, and a current dependency/inventory lineage.
