# Teoyube formative pilot adverse-event protocol

Status: **DRAFT - OWNER/LOCAL SAFETY REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE USE**

## Fixed response order

1. Stop the affected task or session.
2. Address immediate human safety before product or spiritual discussion.
3. Avoid theological interpretation, diagnosis, investigation, or unnecessary detail collection.
4. Direct the participant to appropriate local human, emergency, pastoral, professional, or trusted-person support as applicable.
5. Record only a minimal safe incident using participant ID, category, time, route, action taken, and safe disposition.
6. Notify the approved escalation owner.
7. Disable an affected optional capability through its existing kill switch when required.
8. Preserve privacy-safe evidence and version/hash metadata without raw content.
9. Determine restart criteria through incident review; never resume simply to complete the study.

Do not invent crisis phone numbers, URLs, service availability, or location-specific resources. Use only verified resources during later execution. When immediate danger may exist, use the existing generic policy: encourage local emergency services or the nearest emergency department and a trusted person physically present when safe.

## Event-specific actions

| Event | Stop condition | Immediate response | Product containment | Restart criterion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Immediate danger or self-harm | Any credible immediate-risk disclosure | Stop; encourage immediate local emergency/human support; do not continue questioning | End session; no live provider call | Safety owner confirms research follow-up is appropriate; participant is not resumed in the same session merely for completion |
| Abuse or dangerous situation | Disclosure indicates present danger or unsafe confrontation | Stop; prioritize safe human/professional support; do not direct confrontation | End affected task; minimize notes | Reviewed safe disposition and no continuing research pressure |
| Dangerous divine command | Product or participant frames a harmful command as certain divine instruction | Stop; do not affirm supernatural authority; encourage trusted human and appropriate professional support | Disable live AI if implicated; use deterministic safe response only | Root cause reviewed, synthetic regression added, safety gates pass |
| Severe distress | Observable distress or participant asks to stop | Pause/stop, offer withdrawal and deletion options | End affected task | Participant independently chooses future participation after review; no same-session pressure |
| Privacy or cross-user data | Any unauthorized or other-user information appears | Stop interaction; avoid copying content | Disable affected memory/retrieval capability; preserve metadata only | Isolation fix, deletion verification, security review, and retest pass |
| Fabricated or corrupted Scripture | Displayed citation/text is unverified or changed | Stop spiritual recommendation; explain that the source cannot be relied upon | Fall back to canonical exact Scripture or no quotation | Canonical corpus verification and citation regression pass |
| Coercive spiritual output | Output pressures action, claims divine authority, blame, guaranteed outcome, or spiritual worth | Stop; tell participant not to rely on the output | Disable live AI when implicated; deterministic fallback | Safety/theology review and regression pass |
| Unauthorized memory write | Data is stored without effective purpose consent and explicit action | Stop; revoke/delete affected record and preserve metadata | Disable write path; invalidate affected session if needed | Deletion, consent, isolation, and audit checks pass |
| Exposed secret | Credential, token, or private key appears | Stop and restrict access; never copy into research notes | Disable affected service and rotate/revoke through approved operations | Revocation/rotation verified and secret scan passes |

## Minimal incident record

Allowed: incident ID, participant ID, timestamp, route, task, category, severity, safe error code, whether the session stopped, support/escalation action, kill-switch state, evidence hash, owner, status, and restart decision.

Prohibited: crisis narrative, health details, trauma or abuse narrative, prayer, journal, testimony, raw prompt/response, names, contact details, credentials, or unnecessary screenshots/logs.
