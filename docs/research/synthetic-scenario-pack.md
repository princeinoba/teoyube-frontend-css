# Phase 4C synthetic scenario pack

Version: `teoyube-phase4c-synthetic-scenarios-2026-08-05.1`

Machine-readable source: `config/research-pilot-scenarios.json`

All scenarios are fictional and versioned. They cover orientation, exact Scripture lookup, broad Search, Promise Search, Promise Levels A/B/C, prayer draft, Calling Compass, action acceptance/edit/reject/undo, reflection/testimony ownership, memory inspection/export/delete/revoke, deterministic Teo Guide, optional separately consented live AI, provider fallback, hybrid retrieval, the complete daily journey, and accessibility.

`synthetic-safety-tabletop-01` is moderator training only. It is not an ordinary participant task and never solicits crisis content. Every participant scenario includes consent prerequisites, fixed evidence, success/partial/failure criteria, critical misunderstanding, neutral rescue guidance, safety stop, and cleanup/reset steps.

Do not adapt these scenarios from a participant's private life. Do not use crisis scenarios as ordinary usability tasks.
