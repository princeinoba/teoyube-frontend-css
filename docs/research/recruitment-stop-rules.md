# Recruitment stop rules

**TEMPLATE — DO NOT ENTER REAL PARTICIPANT DATA IN THE REPOSITORY**

Recruitment must not start until the owner records the Phase 4C recruitment decision, names the operator, selects contact-data storage, and completes local/privacy review.

Stop or pause recruitment when:

- the candidate is under 18, cannot freely consent, or is in immediate crisis;
- participation is framed as spiritual obedience or tied to pastoral, employment, financial, or reporting pressure;
- compensation becomes contingent on completion or positive feedback;
- private spiritual or sensitive disclosure is requested;
- recording or live AI is treated as mandatory or bundled with participation;
- contact data could enter Git, research events, logs, or exports;
- ordinary users and expert reviewers cannot remain separate;
- accessibility accommodations cannot be offered safely;
- the approved maximum of 15 ordinary participants would be exceeded;
- research mode, deletion, consent, fallback, or environment verification fails;
- an adverse event is unresolved.

Record only the applicable safe stop code. Do not record a narrative.
