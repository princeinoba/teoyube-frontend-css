# Teoyube formative pilot recruitment screen

Status: **DRAFT - DO NOT RECRUIT UNTIL OWNER APPROVAL**

Use a candidate code rather than a name in this form. Store contact details separately.

1. Are you 18 years of age or older? Yes / No. A `No` response is ineligible for this initial pilot.
2. Are you able to understand the study information and voluntarily consent? Yes / No.
3. Are you interested in Scripture, prayer, calling, spiritual growth, or Christian formation? Yes / No / Prefer not to say.
4. How familiar are you with Bible-study or prayer applications? None / Limited / Comfortable / Extensive.
5. How familiar are you with AI-powered applications? None / Limited / Comfortable / Extensive.
6. How would you describe your general technical confidence? Low / Moderate / High / Prefer not to say.
7. Do you use an accessibility feature or assistive technology that you would be willing to use during the study? Yes / No / Prefer not to say. If yes, record only the accommodation needed for the session, not a diagnosis.
8. Which study language can you comfortably use? Record only the approved study-language choice.
9. Are you available for one 60-75 minute moderated session? Yes / No.
10. Recording preference: no recording / optional audio / optional screen / optional video. Final options depend on the owner decision.
11. Do you have a close reporting, employment, pastoral-authority, or financial relationship with the researcher that could make saying no difficult? Yes / No / Unsure. Escalate for safeguards; do not ask for unnecessary details.
12. Are you currently dealing with an immediate crisis or danger that makes a product research session inappropriate today? Yes / No / Prefer not to say. A `Yes` response ends screening compassionately without requesting details and directs the person toward appropriate human support.
13. Do you agree to use only fictional study scenarios rather than private prayer, journal, testimony, health, trauma, abuse, relationship, crisis, or memory content? Yes / No.

Do not ask for denomination, church membership, diagnosis, crisis narrative, date of birth, full address, or private spiritual history. Recruitment is not authorized until the owner approves the pilot decision and local privacy/legal review is completed where applicable.
