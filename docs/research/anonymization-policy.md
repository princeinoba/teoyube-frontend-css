# Teoyube pilot anonymization, retention, and deletion policy

Status: **OWNER-APPROVED POLICY - OPERATOR AND RETENTION PERIOD REQUIRED BEFORE COLLECTION**

## Separation

- Research analysis uses a random participant ID, never a name or email.
- Recruitment/contact data is stored separately from session results with a separate access list and deletion schedule.
- The linking key, if required for deletion requests, is stored separately and never included in analysis exports.
- Expert reviewers use a distinct segment code and are not combined with ordinary-user usability outcomes.

## Data minimization

Collect only fields in `data-dictionary.md`. Do not collect raw prayer, journal, testimony, memory, check-in, health, trauma, abuse, relationship, crisis, prompt/response, credential, or token content. Redact accidental disclosure immediately; preserve only a safe incident category and action.

Free-text notes must be bounded, non-sensitive, and reviewed before analysis. Quotations may be retained only when non-sensitive, de-identified, necessary for a research finding, and approved under the final consent terms.

## Access and transfer

Access is limited to the owner and approved research operator. Research files remain in the approved local/staging research location. Do not place them in public assets, source-code fixtures, normal telemetry, screenshots, model context, or Git.

## Retention and deletion

- Retention period: **PENDING OWNER DECISION**.
- Optional recordings, if approved, should have a shorter stated retention than analyzed de-identified results.
- Recruitment contact data is deleted separately after scheduling/follow-up needs end.
- A participant may request withdrawal or deletion through the approved contact. Remove identifiable research records and link keys when applicable; document only a content-free deletion status.
- Aggregated findings already stripped of participant linkage may not be technically attributable to one participant; the consent form must explain the final reviewed rule without making unsupported legal claims.

No retention clock, deletion completion, or participant consent may be fabricated in Phase 4A.
