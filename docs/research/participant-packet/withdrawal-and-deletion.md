# Withdrawal and deletion

**OWNER/LOCAL PRIVACY REVIEW DRAFT**

You may pause, skip, or withdraw without penalty. Tell the moderator or use the owner-approved contact process. Withdrawal stops new research recording immediately.

You may request deletion of participant-scoped research events in the active local store. The operator verifies the request through the separate contact process, deletes by the research deletion key, regenerates aggregate exports, and provides the approved safe confirmation. Contact records and optional recordings follow their separately stated deletion processes.

Deletion does not make unsupported claims about material already irreversibly included in a truly de-identified aggregate or unknown backups. Those limits must be explained before a real session.
