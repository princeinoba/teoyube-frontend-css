# Privacy summary

**OWNER/LOCAL PRIVACY REVIEW DRAFT**

The study is designed to record minimal coded outcomes such as completion, a fixed issue code, rescue count, route, duration bucket, and a 1–5 clarity or trust rating. It must not record raw prayer, journal, testimony, reflection, search, prompt, model, health, trauma, abuse, relationship, crisis, contact, credential, or location content.

Names and contact details are kept separately from research results. The research event store is local, ignored by Git, encrypted, participant-scoped, and retained under the approved 180-day class. This is an active-local-store deletion claim only; no unsupported backup-deletion claim is made.

Research participation does not authorize ordinary product memory, recording, live AI, accessibility observation, or follow-up contact.
