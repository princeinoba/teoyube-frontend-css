# Optional recording consent summary

**OWNER/LOCAL PRIVACY REVIEW DRAFT**

Recording is optional and remains pending the owner decision. Audio, screen, and video must be explained and consented separately when offered. Declining recording does not affect eligibility, compensation, or access to the ordinary study.

The participant may stop recording at any time. The operator must state the recording location, access list, retention period, deletion process, and any conferencing-provider implications before consent. No recording is stored in Git or the research event store.
