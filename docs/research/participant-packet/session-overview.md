# What happens in a session

**OWNER/LOCAL PRIVACY REVIEW DRAFT**

The moderated session lasts about 60–75 minutes. You will use fictional scenarios to explore Scripture lookup, Search, Promise Search, prayer drafts, Calling Compass, reversible actions, testimony boundaries, memory controls, Teo Guide, retrieval, accessibility, and the daily journey.

The moderator studies the product, not your faith. There are no right spiritual answers. You may think aloud, remain silent, skip, pause, or withdraw. The moderator may use neutral prompts when the interface is unclear. Private spiritual or sensitive stories are not needed.

An optional live-AI task and optional recording may be offered only after separate consent. Declining either does not end the session.
