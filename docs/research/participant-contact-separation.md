# Participant contact separation

**TEMPLATE — DO NOT ENTER REAL PARTICIPANT DATA IN THE REPOSITORY**

**OWNER/LOCAL PRIVACY REVIEW DRAFT**

Contact information must never enter Git, research events, session envelopes, task score sheets, exports, screenshots, logs, or the `.var/research/events` store.

- The owner must select a separate access-controlled contact location before recruitment.
- Contact records use a candidate code. Research records use a separately generated participant code. The crosswalk is kept only in the approved contact location.
- The research operator receives only the minimum scheduling details needed for the session.
- Follow-up contact requires `research_follow_up_contact` consent and is independent of participation, recording, accessibility observation, memory, and live-AI consent.
- Contact data and compensation administration never contain task results, spiritual content, accessibility diagnosis, or adverse-event narratives.
- Deletion schedules and access lists are maintained separately for contact and research data.

The repository may contain templates and policy only. It may not contain real contact rows, crosswalks, participant IDs, consent signatures, or scheduling records.
