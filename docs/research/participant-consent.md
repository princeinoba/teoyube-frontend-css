# Teoyube formative pilot participant consent draft

**DRAFT - OWNER/LOCAL PRIVACY REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE USE**

## Why this research is being done

Teoyube is a local/staging research product that helps people work with Scripture, prayer, calling discernment, daily actions, reflection, and user-controlled memory. This study asks whether the product is understandable, useful, trustworthy, accessible, and appropriately humble.

This research does not test your faith, spiritual maturity, holiness, or whether God acted through the product.

## Your choice

Participation is voluntary. You may skip any question or task, pause, or withdraw without penalty. Compensation, if offered under the approved pilot design, is fixed and does not depend on completing every task or giving positive feedback.

## What you will do

During a 60-75 minute moderated session, you will use supplied fictional scenarios to explore Teoyube, inspect Scripture and explanations, try reversible actions, review memory controls, and discuss your understanding. An optional task may use a synthetic message with a live AI provider. You may decline that task and still participate.

## Data collected

The study may collect a participant ID, segment, age band, volunteered accessibility accommodations, session date, moderator, task outcomes, rescue counts, safe error codes, routes, useful task timing, comprehension answers, clarity/trust ratings, non-sensitive feedback, accessibility barriers, adverse-event flag, and recording-consent choice.

Names and recruitment contact details must be stored separately from research results.

## Data not collected

Please do not share private prayers, journal entries, testimony, memories, check-in text, health information, trauma or abuse narratives, relationship details, crisis narratives, credentials, API keys, tokens, or personal live-AI prompts. The moderator will stop and redirect if private content begins to enter the research record.

## Optional recording

Recording is **PENDING OWNER DECISION**. If recording is offered, audio, screen, and video permission must each be explained and consented to separately. Declining recording does not prevent participation. Recording may be stopped at any time.

## Optional external AI processing

The live-AI task is **PENDING OWNER DECISION**. If included, it uses a synthetic, non-sensitive message. OpenAI may process that message only after a separate consent check. Teoyube requests `store: false` but does not claim verified Zero Data Retention. You may decline or revoke consent and continue with deterministic Teoyube behavior.

## Risks and safeguards

Possible risks include confusion, discomfort with spiritual or AI language, accidental disclosure, accessibility barriers, or distress. The moderator will use synthetic scenarios, avoid theological correction during tasks, minimize records, and follow the adverse-event protocol. Benefits are not guaranteed.

Teoyube is not a replacement for Scripture, prayer, pastoral care, church community, medical or mental-health care, legal or financial advice, or emergency services.

## Privacy, retention, and deletion

Research results use participant IDs rather than names. Access is limited to the approved research operator and owner. Retention duration is **PENDING OWNER DECISION**. Contact/recruitment records are kept separately and deleted on their own schedule. You may request withdrawal or deletion of identifiable research material using the approved contact below, subject to the final reviewed policy and any already aggregated de-identified findings.
