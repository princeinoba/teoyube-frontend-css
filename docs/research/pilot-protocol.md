# Teoyube formative pilot protocol

Status: **DRAFT - OWNER DECISION REQUIRED BEFORE RECRUITMENT**

Protocol version: `teoyube-formative-pilot-1.0.0`

## Purpose

This moderated formative study is designed to learn whether adults can understand, trust, and safely use Teoyube's Scripture-rooted formation experience. It evaluates comprehension, voluntary action, reversibility, continuity, accessibility, and perceived spiritual usefulness. It does not test whether God acted through the product and does not create a holiness, faith, favor, or spiritual score.

The product remains unchanged during Phase 4A. No participant exists, no recruitment is authorized, and no research instrumentation is enabled.

## Product and theology commitments

The study must preserve and test these boundaries:

- Scripture is the highest authority.
- Exact Scripture, textual context, Teoyube interpretation, prayer, application, calling evidence, and testimony are distinct.
- Promise Levels A, B, and C remain distinct.
- Calling is discerned humbly over time and is never declared as divine certainty by AI.
- Only the user may confirm testimony or promise fulfillment.
- Memory is user-owned, purpose-specific, inspectable, revocable, exportable, and deletable.
- Durable actions require explicit confirmation and remain reviewable, rejectable, and reversible.
- Crisis and sensitive-topic behavior prioritizes human safety and appropriate support.
- Teoyube does not replace Scripture, prayer, pastoral care, church community, medical or mental-health care, legal or financial professionals, or emergency services.

## Recommended design

- Pilot type: moderated formative usability and trust study.
- Recommended sample: 12 adults; minimum 8 and maximum 15.
- Segments: 4-5 users familiar with Christian Bible study or prayer apps; 3-4 users with general Christian experience and limited spiritual-app experience; 2-3 users representing accessibility or assistive-technology needs.
- Expert review: optional 1-2 pastors, mentors, ministry leaders, or mature Christian reviewers, analyzed separately.
- Age: 18 and older. Minors are excluded from this initial pilot.
- Mode: remote, local, or mixed moderated sessions in a local/staging research environment.
- Duration: 60-75 minutes.
- Compensation: fixed, non-coercive, and independent of positive feedback if offered.
- Recording: optional and separately consented.
- Live AI: optional; requires separate external-processing consent and a synthetic non-sensitive message.
- Sensitive content: synthetic scenarios only.

The 8-15 participant range supports early discovery of repeated usability and trust problems without presenting the study as statistically representative. Accessibility participants provide direct barrier evidence, while expert reviewers provide theological and ministry feedback without substituting for ordinary-user usability. The owner should obtain applicable local legal and privacy advice before recruitment; this protocol is not legal, clinical, or institutional-review-board advice.

## Eligibility and exclusions

Include adults able to consent who are interested in Scripture, prayer, calling, spiritual growth, or Christian formation. Seek varied technical confidence, AI familiarity, Teoyube-concept familiarity, and accessibility needs.

Exclude minors, people unable to provide informed consent, anyone currently in immediate crisis, anyone being directed to use Teoyube instead of professional or emergency care, and anyone whose reporting or financial relationship could make participation coercive unless documented safeguards are approved. Participants are never required to disclose private spiritual, health, trauma, abuse, relationship, or crisis details.

## Environment and session flow

1. Confirm consent and recording preference.
2. Remind the participant to use only the supplied synthetic scenarios.
3. Establish think-aloud expectations and the right to skip or withdraw.
4. Run the 15 tasks in the task guide, varying order only when documented for fatigue or accessibility.
5. Check consent again before the optional live-AI task.
6. Stop immediately if the adverse-event protocol is triggered.
7. Complete the neutral debrief and explain data deletion contact/process.
8. Store only the minimum de-identified research record.

## Decision outcomes

- `PROCEED_TO_REMEDIATION_OR_NEXT_PILOT`: thresholds met and no unresolved zero-tolerance issue.
- `ITERATE_AND_RETEST`: ordinary usability or comprehension findings require scoped changes and another test.
- `STOP_FOR_SAFETY_PRIVACY_OR_ACCESSIBILITY`: a zero-tolerance or high-impact issue remains unresolved.

No pilot result authorizes production launch, a visual change, a theological claim, or product instrumentation by itself.
