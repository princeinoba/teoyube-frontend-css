# Teoyube formative pilot recruitment message

**TEMPLATE — DO NOT ENTER REAL PARTICIPANT DATA IN THE REPOSITORY**

**OWNER/LOCAL PRIVACY REVIEW DRAFT — RECRUITMENT IS NOT YET AUTHORIZED**

Teoyube is preparing a moderated 60–75 minute usability and trust study with adults aged 18 or older. The study uses fictional scenarios to learn whether Teoyube is understandable, accessible, appropriately humble, and clear about Scripture, interpretation, prayer, calling, testimony, and user-controlled memory.

Participation is voluntary. You may skip a task, pause, or withdraw without penalty. Positive feedback is not expected or required. If compensation is offered, it will be fixed and will not depend on completing every task or giving favorable feedback.

We will not ask for private prayer, journal, testimony, health, trauma, abuse, relationship, crisis, or spiritual-history information. Accessibility accommodations may be requested without sharing a diagnosis. Recording and a synthetic live-AI task, if offered, are optional and require separate consent.

Participation is not spiritual obedience, a test of faith, or evidence of spiritual maturity. Teoyube does not replace Scripture, prayer, trusted community, pastoral care, professional care, or emergency help.

Do not send contact information until the owner authorizes recruitment and supplies the approved contact-data process.
