# Teoyube formative pilot analysis plan

Status: **DRAFT - OWNER DECISION REQUIRED BEFORE USE**

## Analysis groups

Analyze these streams separately before synthesis:

1. Quantitative task outcomes.
2. Qualitative comprehension and trust themes.
3. Accessibility findings.
4. Safety and adverse events.
5. Theological and authority misunderstandings.
6. Privacy, consent, and memory misunderstandings.
7. Product fragmentation and feature redundancy.
8. Trust, voluntariness, and reversibility.
9. Cross-module continuity.

Ordinary participants and expert reviewers remain separate. Expert theological approval cannot substitute for ordinary-user usability or accessibility evidence.

## Required measures

- task completion and partial completion;
- completion without moderator rescue;
- rescue count and critical rescue;
- critical misunderstanding;
- exact Scripture versus interpretation distinction;
- source inspection and explanation comprehension;
- Promise Level comprehension;
- calling humility/uncertainty comprehension;
- testimony/fulfillment ownership comprehension;
- consent and memory comprehension;
- reject/edit/skip/undo success;
- deterministic/live-AI/fallback understanding;
- accessibility blocker count and severity;
- clarity rating;
- trust rating;
- perceived spiritual usefulness rating;
- adverse-event count and disposition;
- cross-module continuity failures;
- fragmentation or redundancy themes.

Time-to-task-completion may diagnose friction but is not a primary success or engagement metric. Do not create a composite spiritual score.

## Recommended formative thresholds

| Outcome | Threshold |
| --- | ---: |
| Core journey completion without critical moderator rescue | at least 80% |
| Exact Scripture versus interpretation understanding | at least 90% |
| Source inspection success | at least 85% |
| Calling humility/uncertainty understanding | at least 90% |
| Testimony and fulfillment ownership understanding | at least 90% |
| Memory-consent comprehension | at least 85% |
| Reject/undo task success | at least 90% |
| Provider fallback understanding | at least 80% |
| Critical safety misunderstanding | 0 tolerated |
| Cross-user/privacy failure | 0 tolerated |
| Fabricated citation observed | 0 tolerated |
| Unresolved coercion or divine-authority impression | 0 tolerated |
| Unresolved high-impact accessibility blockers before broad launch | 0 tolerated |

These thresholds are proposed pilot decision rules, not universal scientific truths. They require owner approval before use.

## Coding and review

- Use a predefined codebook for authority confusion, source comprehension, calling certainty, consent/memory, reversibility, continuity, fragmentation, accessibility, safety, trust, and usefulness.
- Two reviewers independently code severe safety, authority, privacy, coercion, and accessibility findings where staffing permits.
- Resolve disagreements by reviewing only the minimum de-identified evidence and recording the reason for the final code.
- Report numerator, denominator, missing/skipped tasks, and confidence limitations. Do not overgeneralize from a formative sample.
- Segment only when consented and large enough to avoid re-identification.

## Decision rule

Any zero-tolerance finding blocks expansion until fixed and retested. Other misses lead to scoped remediation and another formative cycle. Meeting thresholds supports a pilot conclusion only; it does not authorize production launch or a 9/10 claim by itself.
