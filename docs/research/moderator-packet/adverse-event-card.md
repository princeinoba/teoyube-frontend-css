# Adverse-event card

**KEEP AVAILABLE DURING EVERY SESSION**

1. Stop the task or session.
2. Prioritize immediate human safety.
3. Do not diagnose, investigate, interpret spiritually, or collect a narrative.
4. Encourage appropriate local emergency, professional, pastoral, trusted-person, or community support as applicable.
5. Record only participant code, safe category, time bucket, route, action, and disposition.
6. Notify the approved escalation owner.
7. Withdraw or delete research data when requested through the approved process.
8. Do not resume until consent and the reviewed restart criteria are satisfied.

Critical distress or crisis always stops the research session. Teoyube is not emergency or professional care.
