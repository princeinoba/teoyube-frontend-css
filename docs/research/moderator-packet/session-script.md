# Moderator session script

**OWNER/LOCAL PRIVACY REVIEW DRAFT**

1. “We are testing Teoyube, not you or your faith. Positive feedback is not required.”
2. Confirm participation and product-event consent. Confirm optional recording separately.
3. “Please use only the supplied fictional scenarios. Do not share private prayer, journal, testimony, health, trauma, abuse, relationship, crisis, or memory details.”
4. “You may skip, pause, or withdraw at any time without penalty.”
5. Use the scenario wording without explaining or defending the interface.
6. Rescue hierarchy: repeat; ask what options are visible; point to a region; name the control; stop for safety. Record only the rescue level and code.
7. Do not correct theology during usability tasks unless safety or a critical authority misunderstanding requires a neutral boundary reminder.
8. Before optional live AI, reconfirm both optional live-AI and external-processing consent. Never enable it by default.
9. Stop on distress, crisis, private disclosure, consent uncertainty, or an unresolved safety failure.
10. Debrief neutrally; explain withdrawal/deletion, recording disposition, and follow-up choice.
