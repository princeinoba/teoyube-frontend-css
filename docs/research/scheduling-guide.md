# Phase 4C scheduling guide

**TEMPLATE — DO NOT ENTER REAL PARTICIPANT DATA IN THE REPOSITORY**

**OWNER/LOCAL PRIVACY REVIEW DRAFT — DO NOT SCHEDULE UNTIL RECRUITMENT IS AUTHORIZED**

1. Confirm the candidate is an adult and eligible under the approved screen.
2. Offer a 60–75 minute slot without pressure or spiritual framing.
3. Ask only what accommodation is useful; do not request a diagnosis.
4. Explain that recording and live AI are optional and separately consented.
5. Send the participant packet without including a participant code in Git-backed material.
6. Keep the contact record in the owner-approved separate location.
7. Assign ordinary-user and expert-reviewer sessions to distinct cohort schedules and analysis streams.
8. Leave recovery time between sessions and do not overbook the moderator.
9. Cancel or reschedule without penalty when consent, privacy, accessibility, safety, or environment readiness is uncertain.
10. Never schedule someone in immediate crisis; end screening compassionately without collecting details.
