# Sensitive-topic taxonomy

Version: `teoyube-sensitive-topic-taxonomy-1.0.0`

The taxonomy is routing support, not diagnosis and not verification of a spiritual claim. Multiple matches use the highest severity; inputs exceeding limits use the typed unknown/multi-topic high-severity fallback.

| Topic | Default severity | Mode | Non-negotiable boundary |
|---|---:|---|---|
| ordinary spiritual question | ordinary | ordinary guidance | Scripture and interpretation stay distinct |
| doubt | moderate | sensitive support | no shame or salvation threat |
| grief | moderate | sensitive support | acknowledge loss; no hidden-cause claim |
| trauma | high | professional support | preserve control; no forced disclosure |
| abuse | high | professional support | safety and independent support; never remain in danger |
| self-harm/immediate danger | critical | immediate safety | human safety before Scripture; no secrecy promise |
| psychosis/paranoia/divine commands | high | professional support | validate distress, never the supernatural belief |
| spiritual coercion | high | professional support | name fear, secrecy, isolation, forced obedience as unsafe |
| relationship crisis | moderate | sensitive support | distinguish conflict from abuse; no divine marriage decision |
| medical concern | high | professional support | no diagnosis or medication change |
| financial desperation | high | professional support | no prosperity, gambling, risky giving, borrowing, or investment direction |
| legal concern | high | professional support | no representation, strategy, or guarantee |
| prophecy/divine messages | moderate | sensitive support | reported experience, tested humbly over Scripture/time/fruit/counsel |
| demonic interpretation | high | professional support | no possession/causation confirmation or dangerous deliverance |
| scrupulosity/compulsive religious fear | high | professional support | no reassurance loop or exhaustive sin checking |
| sexual/domestic violence | critical | immediate safety | no confrontation/submission requirement or victim blame |
| substance/behavioral crisis | high | professional support | overdose/dangerous withdrawal escalates; no detox instruction |
| unknown/multi-topic sensitive content | high | professional support | highest severity wins; ask only minimum safety information |

Immediate-danger signals escalate applicable topics to critical. Topic detection is bounded to four topics and never creates a persistent crisis or mental-health profile.
