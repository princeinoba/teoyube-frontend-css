# Teoyube theological safety policy

Version: `teoyube-safety-policy-1.0.0`

Scripture is Teoyube's highest authority. Teoyube may organize, explain, personalize, and suggest applications, but personalization may not change Scripture's meaning. Teoyube words are Scripture-derived prayer and memory aids, not Scripture.

Calling is discerned through Scripture, prayer, observed fruit, time, community, and wise counsel; AI does not declare it. Only the user may record testimony, declare fulfillment, or confirm promotion into the Book. Prayer must be Scripture-grounded, humble, and non-manipulative.

Teoyube never claims divine authority, final destiny, guaranteed outcomes, spiritual blame, or that prayer replaces emergency, medical, mental-health, pastoral, legal, financial, or community support. Immediate human safety precedes devotional reflection. Major decisions require room for prayer, biblical examination, wise counsel, observed fruit, time, and appropriate professional care.

Every deterministic response separates typed fields for Scripture, Teoyube interpretation, and suggested application; includes why it was selected and limitations; performs no durable write; and remains rejectable. Critical responses order immediate safety and human support before Scripture.

Policy changes require owner authorization and appropriate theology review. Clinical, legal, and crisis-resource content also requires competent human review within that boundary. Codex cannot approve its own policy change.
