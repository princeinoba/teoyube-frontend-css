# Scripture licensing and display policy

- `FULL_TEXT_ALLOWED`: exact text may be returned only when translation, source, version, checksums, attribution, and owner display approval are registered.
- `REFERENCE_ONLY`: only canonical references may be returned.
- `EXCERPT_LIMITED`: text is restricted to a recorded excerpt and attribution policy.
- `DISPLAY_BLOCKED_LICENSE_UNKNOWN`: text cannot be rendered, validated, indexed, or used as context.

The registered `engwebp` World English Bible corpus is public domain and `FULL_TEXT_ALLOWED` under decision `TEOYUBE-OWNER-SCRIPTURE-QUOTATION-2026-07-20-P15B`. Displayed verbatim quotations use exact corpus wording and the `WEB` abbreviation. The World English Bible name is reserved for faithful copies, so Teoyube does not shorten, harmonize, or rewrite its wording.

PGP signature verification was **NOT PERFORMED** because GPG was unavailable. The owner accepted this residual risk only for archive SHA-256 `4253589697DC6B5E92695655F2F28792D50E7BE7B9C8E212AF4F4BD18E866C3B`. Teoyube must not claim independent cryptographic publisher-signature verification.

The legacy KJV excerpts remain `DISPLAY_BLOCKED_LICENSE_UNKNOWN`. Prayer, paraphrase, interpretation, promise level, calling language, and user-authored content never inherit a WEB label merely because they have grounding references.
