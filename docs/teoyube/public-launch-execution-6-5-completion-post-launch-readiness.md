# Public Launch Execution 6.5 - Public Launch Completion Review & Post-Launch Readiness

Status: Complete

Public Launch Execution 6.5 adds the public launch completion review and post-launch readiness layer. It reviews the completed public launch execution systems, summarizes public feedback readiness, confirms issue closure and stabilization evidence, certifies final safety/privacy posture, and prepares a post-launch readiness handoff.

## What This Adds

- Completion contracts.
- Public launch completion review.
- Public launch feedback summary.
- Public launch issue closure.
- Public launch stability certification.
- Final public launch safety/privacy review.
- Post-launch readiness criteria.
- Post-launch readiness package.
- Post-launch risk register.
- Post-launch known limitations.
- Post-launch owner readiness review.
- Post-launch readiness handoff.
- Public launch completion audit.
- Example and smoke check.
- Documentation.

## Public Launch Completion Review

The completion review checks that controlled public activation, public launch-day monitoring, manual feedback intake, public feedback triage, public fix queue, public daily review, public safe-fix release, stabilization regression, post-release safety verification, and surface stabilization exist. It also verifies that unresolved blockers are visible and that public-launch-critical, privacy/terms/consent, Scripture anchor, explanation path, fallback, consent/privacy, and mobile/accessibility blockers are resolved or documented.

If no real public launch was recorded in the codebase, the review does not claim one happened. It records a readiness-only completion state.

## Feedback Summary

The feedback summary uses manually entered, sanitized, redacted feedback notes only. It summarizes positive feedback, critical feedback, feature requests, content clarity issues, privacy/terms/consent feedback, Scripture anchor feedback, explanation path feedback, fallback feedback, mobile/accessibility feedback, AI Companion feedback, and personalization preview feedback.

It does not collect, send, persist, or fetch feedback automatically.

## Issue Closure

Issue closure only considers an issue closed when it has a fix result or accepted manual resolution, required regression checks are complete or documented, no safety guardrail was weakened, privacy/terms/consent notices remain available where required, Scripture anchors remain required, explanation paths remain required, fallback remains safe, consent controls remain visible, and no restricted service was enabled.

## Stability Certification

The stability certification confirms public surfaces remain usable, critical feedback is not unresolved, the public fix queue has no unresolved launch blockers, regression checks are complete or documented, daily reviews do not recommend rollback, pause/continue decision support is stable, production service status is known and safe, and owner review exists when required.

## Final Safety/Privacy Review

The final safety/privacy review confirms Scripture anchoring remains required, explanation paths remain required, fallback remains enabled and non-empty, confidence is not overstated, divine certainty claims are absent, consent controls remain available, privacy/terms/consent notices remain available, personalization remains consent-aware, raw sensitive text storage remains disabled, hidden personalization is not introduced, unapproved analytics remain disabled, unapproved production persistence remains disabled, live AI orchestration remains disabled, debug UI remains hidden from normal users, and offline fallback remains Scripture-anchored.

## Post-Launch Readiness Criteria

Post-launch readiness criteria check that Public Launch Execution is complete, completion review is complete, feedback summary is complete, issue closure is complete, stability certification is complete, final safety/privacy review is complete, final surface readiness is complete, no unresolved public-launch-critical blockers remain, known limitations are documented, owner review is available, production service status is explicit and controlled, and unapproved persistence, analytics, and live AI orchestration are not accidentally enabled.

## Readiness Package

The readiness package combines the completion report, feedback summary report, issue closure report, stability certification report, final safety/privacy report, post-launch readiness criteria report, surface readiness report, production service status, known limitations, risk register, owner review, and recommended next stage.

The package remains in memory only and is not sent or stored externally.

## Risk Register

The post-launch risk register tracks manual risks across privacy, terms, consent, Scripture anchors, explanation paths, fallback, confidence, mobile, accessibility, performance, feedback, content clarity, AI Companion, personalization, offline, security, production services, and unknown categories.

## Known Limitations

Known limitations document that public launch completion review does not mean all future services are connected, database persistence may still require explicit connection later, analytics may still require explicit connection later, live AI orchestration may still require explicit connection later, personalization remains consent-aware, users should not submit sensitive personal information unless future privacy terms and storage rules are finalized, Scripture anchors and explanation paths remain required, fallback paths remain enabled, and some features may remain limited until post-launch improvements are complete.

## Owner Readiness Review

Owner readiness review covers public launch completion, public feedback summary, issue closure, stability certification, final safety/privacy review, known limitations, post-launch risks, readiness package, production service status, and whether post-launch operations may begin or are blocked.

## Post-Launch Readiness Handoff

The handoff prepares Post-Launch Operations 7.1: public monitoring roadmap, support workflow, manual feedback review workflow, public issue triage workflow, production service decision roadmap, analytics/persistence/live AI future decision points, privacy/legal follow-up requirements, and next action checklist.

## What This Does Not Include

This step does not include:

- Automatic user contact.
- Automatic feedback collection.
- Public URL fetching.
- Unapproved database persistence.
- External analytics sending.
- Live AI orchestration.
- Service worker implementation.
- Native mobile app build.
- Paid infrastructure.
- Production monitoring connection.
