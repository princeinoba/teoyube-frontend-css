# Post-Launch Operations 7.2 - Support Desk, Feedback Review & Weekly Improvement Loop

Status: Complete

Post-Launch Operations 7.2 adds a safe manual operating loop for public support notes, weekly feedback review, issue-to-fix planning, owner review, and next-week improvement prioritization. It builds on 7.1 public monitoring and keeps all support and feedback activity sanitized, owner-reviewable, and in memory.

## What This Adds

- Support desk contracts.
- Manual support desk workflow.
- Weekly feedback review.
- Weekly improvement loop.
- Support issue-to-fix bridge.
- Weekly owner review.
- Weekly improvement package.
- Post-Launch Operations 7.2 audit.
- Example, smoke check, export, launch audit, quality gate, and roadmap/status updates.

## Support Desk Workflow

The support desk workflow accepts only manually supplied support ticket summaries. It classifies tickets by support category, sanitizes notes, flags emergency, crisis, medical, legal, financial, sensitive-information, Scripture, explanation, fallback, privacy, consent, mobile, accessibility, and personalization concerns for manual review, and avoids professional advice.

It does not contact users, persist tickets, fetch public URLs, send analytics, call providers, enable live AI, or store raw sensitive text by default.

## Feedback Review Loop

The weekly feedback review organizes manually supplied feedback into themes for Scripture clarity, promise relevance, prayer usefulness, Calling Compass clarity, AI Companion clarity, mobile usability, accessibility, consent/privacy clarity, fallback quality, content clarity, and feature requests.

It does not collect feedback automatically. Feedback items remain redacted summaries for owner review.

## Weekly Improvement Loop

The weekly improvement loop creates structured priorities for Scripture coverage, Promise Cluster coverage, Prayer Companion improvements, Calling Compass improvements, TIG Response Panel improvements, TIG Graph Explorer improvements, WordCard clarity, mobile polish, accessibility polish, public copy clarity, feedback/consent clarity, and performance.

Every improvement item must preserve Scripture anchors, explanation paths, fallback safety, consent controls, privacy notices, consent-aware personalization, provider boundaries, secrets, and spiritual-content humility.

## Issue-To-Fix Bridge

The support issue-to-fix bridge converts eligible support tickets into weekly fix candidates. It blocks unsafe requests that would remove Scripture anchors, remove explanation paths, weaken fallback safety, hide consent controls, enable hidden personalization, enable unapproved analytics, enable unapproved database persistence, enable live AI orchestration, expose secrets, or claim divine certainty.

## Owner Review

The weekly owner review records whether support tickets, feedback themes, Scripture/explanation issues, fallback issues, privacy/consent issues, mobile/accessibility issues, weekly fix candidates, deferred improvements, and next-week priorities were reviewed and accepted.

Owner review is manual-only and performs no user contact or external write.

## Weekly Improvement Package

The weekly improvement package combines the support desk report, weekly feedback review, weekly improvement plan, support issue-to-fix bridge report, owner review, and next action recommendation.

The package is in-memory only. It is not sent or stored externally.

## This Step Does Not Include

- Automatic support messaging.
- Automatic feedback collection.
- External analytics.
- Database persistence.
- Live AI orchestration.
- Hidden personalization.
- Public URL fetching.
- Service workers.
- Automatic user contact.
- Professional medical, legal, or financial advice.

## Safety And Privacy Boundaries

Post-Launch Operations 7.2 preserves:

- Scripture anchoring.
- Explanation paths.
- Fallback handling.
- Confidence and no-divine-certainty boundaries.
- Public privacy, terms, consent, sensitive-information, and feedback notices.
- Consent controls.
- Consent-aware personalization.
- Manual support and feedback review.
- Redacted, sanitized summaries.
