# Limited Soft Launch Execution 4.5 - Soft Launch Completion Review & Public Launch Readiness

Limited Soft Launch Execution 4.5 adds the completion review and public launch readiness layer for Teoyube. It reviews the completed limited soft launch execution systems, summarizes manual feedback, confirms issue closure and stabilization evidence, certifies final safety/privacy posture, and prepares a public launch readiness handoff.

This step is a review, readiness, certification, and handoff layer only. It does not publicly launch Teoyube.

## What This Step Adds

- Completion contracts
- Soft launch completion review
- Manual feedback summary
- Issue closure review
- Stability certification
- Final safety/privacy review
- Public launch readiness criteria
- Public launch readiness package
- Public launch risk register
- Public launch known limitations
- Owner readiness review
- Public launch readiness handoff
- Soft launch completion audit
- Example
- Smoke check
- Documentation

## Completion Review

The completion review checks that Limited Soft Launch Execution 4.1, 4.2, 4.3, and 4.4 systems exist and remain ready. It also checks that unresolved blockers, launch-critical issues, Scripture anchor blockers, explanation path blockers, fallback blockers, consent/privacy blockers, and mobile/accessibility blockers are visible before public launch preparation.

## Feedback Summary

The feedback summary uses manual, sanitized, redacted feedback items only. It supports positive feedback, feature requests, content clarity feedback, Scripture anchor feedback, explanation path feedback, fallback feedback, consent/privacy feedback, mobile/accessibility feedback, AI Companion feedback, and personalization preview feedback.

It does not collect feedback automatically, send feedback externally, persist feedback, or store raw sensitive text by default.

## Issue Closure

Issue closure only treats an issue as closed when a fix result or accepted manual resolution exists, required regression checks are complete or documented, safety guardrails are not weakened, Scripture anchors remain required, explanation paths remain required, fallback remains safe, consent controls remain visible, and restricted services are not enabled.

## Stability Certification

Stability certification confirms runtime stability, surface stability, feedback stability, fix queue stability, regression stability, daily review posture, and owner review availability before public launch preparation.

## Final Safety And Privacy Review

The final safety/privacy review confirms Scripture anchoring, explanation paths, fallback behavior, confidence safety, consent controls, privacy boundaries, disabled raw sensitive text storage, no hidden personalization, no external analytics, no production persistence, no live AI orchestration, hidden debug UI, and Scripture-anchored offline fallback.

## Public Launch Readiness Criteria

Public launch readiness criteria confirm that limited soft launch execution is complete, completion review is complete, feedback summary is complete, issue closure is complete, stability certification is complete, final safety/privacy review is complete, surface readiness has no blockers, known limitations are documented, owner review is available, and production service connection plans remain explicit and controlled.

## Public Launch Readiness Package

The readiness package combines completion review, feedback summary, issue closure, stability certification, final safety/privacy review, readiness criteria, surface readiness, known limitations, risks, owner review, and the recommended next stage.

The package remains manual and in-memory only. It is not sent or stored externally.

## Risk Register

The public launch risk register remains in memory only. It supports Scripture anchor, explanation path, fallback, consent, privacy, mobile, accessibility, performance, feedback, content clarity, AI Companion, personalization, offline, security, production services, and unknown risk categories.

## Known Limitations

Known limitations clearly state that public launch has not occurred yet, production persistence may require explicit connection later, external analytics may require explicit connection later, live AI orchestration may require explicit connection later, personalization remains consent-aware, users should not submit sensitive information unless future privacy terms and storage rules are finalized, Scripture anchors and explanation paths remain required, fallback paths remain enabled, and some features may remain limited until public launch preparation is complete.

## Owner Readiness Review

Owner readiness review covers completion review, feedback summary, issue closure, stability certification, final safety/privacy, known limitations, public launch risks, readiness package, external analytics posture, production persistence posture, live AI posture, and the decision to begin or block public launch preparation.

## Readiness Handoff

The handoff prepares Public Launch Preparation 5.1 with a public launch preparation roadmap, production service connection plan, production persistence decision path, analytics connection decision path, live AI orchestration decision path, public QA requirements, privacy/legal content review requirements, support workflow requirements, and final public launch go/no-go requirements.
