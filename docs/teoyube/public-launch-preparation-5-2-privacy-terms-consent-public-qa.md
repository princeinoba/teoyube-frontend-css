## Draft Copy

The privacy notice draft explains that Teoyube is Scripture-centered and explanation-based, personalization remains consent-aware, raw sensitive text is not stored by default, production persistence is not connected, external analytics are not connected, live AI orchestration is not enabled, and Scripture anchors and explanation paths are part of the transparency model.

The terms draft states that Teoyube provides Scripture-centered encouragement and educational/devotional support, does not claim divine certainty, does not provide medical, legal, financial, emergency, or professional counseling advice, and requires human/legal review before public launch.

Consent copy explains personalization preview, session-only personalization, feedback consent, disabled analytics/persistence/live AI placeholders, personalization disable behavior, and reset/export/delete simulations.

AI/TIG transparency copy explains the Teoyube Intelligence Graph, Scripture anchors, explanation paths, confidence labels, fallback behavior, personalization preview transparency, and the fact that live AI orchestration is not enabled.

Sensitive information warnings tell users not to submit highly sensitive personal information, not to use Teoyube for emergencies, and to seek emergency or professional help where needed.

Feedback notice copy explains that feedback may be manually reviewed, should not include sensitive personal information, is not hidden personalization by default, and may help improve clarity, safety, layout, Scripture visibility, explanation paths, and fallback behavior.

## Public QA

The public QA checklist covers Canon, Daily Word, Prayer, Calling Compass, Promise Cluster, AI Companion, Onboarding, TIG Response Panel, TIG Graph Preview, Personalization Preview Panel, Consent Controls, Feedback Controls, Privacy/Terms surfaces, Sensitive Information Warnings, Error/Fallback States, Mobile Navigation, and Offline Fallback.

Each surface is prepared for mobile layout, accessibility basics, Scripture anchor visibility, explanation path visibility, fallback readiness, confidence label clarity, consent visibility, privacy copy availability, sensitive information warning availability, debug payload safety, and no external service dependency for safe render.
