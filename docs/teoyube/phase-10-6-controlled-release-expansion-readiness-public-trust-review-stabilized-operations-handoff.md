# Phase 10.6 - Controlled Release Expansion Readiness, Public Trust Review & Stabilized Operations Handoff

Phase 10.6 creates Teoyube's controlled expansion readiness and stabilized operations handoff system. It is a local-only set of TypeScript helpers and documentation for manual owner review. It does not expand release access, deploy the app, monitor users, collect analytics, contact users, fetch public URLs, or connect external services.

## Prerequisites From Phase 10.5

- First-week stabilization structure exists.
- Manual feedback loop structure exists.
- Repeated issue pattern review exists.
- Known issue register exists.
- Safe-fix batch review exists.
- Controlled release expansion decision structure exists.
- Phase 10.5 owner review exists.
- Build/typecheck/local verification remains the release gate.

## Controlled Release Expansion Readiness Process

The expansion readiness helper checks first-week stabilization, manual feedback, issue patterns, known issues, safe-fix batches, Severity 1 and Severity 2 status, private/secret exposure, unsafe spiritual response patterns, rollback readiness, support expectations, public trust, owner approval, Scripture anchors, explanation traces, fallback safety, confidence labels, privacy/consent boundaries, known limitations, and service-disabled state.

## Public Trust Review Process

Public trust review confirms:

- Teoyube does not claim divine certainty.
- Teoyube does not present spiritual guidance as guaranteed prophecy.
- Teoyube does not replace pastoral, medical, legal, financial, emergency, or professional counseling support.
- Known limitations are visible or documented.
- Privacy and consent notices are visible where required.
- Confidence labels remain visible where required.
- Explanation traces remain available where required.
- Scripture anchors are preserved.
- Fallback states are safe and clear.
- Disabled services are clearly explained.
- Controlled release status is clear.
- Support and feedback expectations are clear.
- Hidden personalization is not introduced.

## Known Limitations Readiness Process

Known limitations readiness checks controlled release status, unfinished feature clarity, disabled service clarity, live AI limitations, manual feedback process, support expectations, spiritual guidance boundaries, emergency/professional advice limitations, known issue visibility to owner review, and no hidden tracking dependency.

## Public Safety Boundary Review Process

Public safety boundary review blocks expansion if Scripture anchors are removed, explanation traces are removed, fallback safety is weakened, confidence labels are hidden, privacy/consent notices are bypassed, known limitations are hidden, hidden personalization is introduced, divine certainty is claimed, authoritative professional advice is provided, external services are added without review, analytics/tracking is introduced without review, database persistence is introduced without review, or unreviewed theological production content is added.

## Stabilized Operations Handoff Process

Stabilized operations handoff checks release owner, manual monitoring cadence, manual feedback review cadence, issue triage process, known issue register maintenance, safe-fix batch process, rollback process, public trust review, weekly improvement loop, remaining limitations, and owner acceptance of the next operations phase.

## Controlled Expansion Gate Process

The controlled expansion gate requires expansion readiness review, public trust pass, known limitations review, public safety boundary pass, stabilized operations handoff review, rollback readiness, no unresolved Severity 1 issue, no unapproved expansion-blocking Severity 2 issue, owner approval, expansion level selection, and documented conditions.

Expansion levels are remain limited, expand to small public group, expand to wider public group, pause expansion, and rollback required. Code records the gate only; it does not perform expansion.

## Stabilized Operations Runbook Process

The runbook records:

- daily manual check process
- weekly feedback review process
- weekly known issue review process
- safe-fix batch review process
- rollback review process
- owner decision log process
- public trust review refresh process
- Severity 1 and Severity 2 escalation process
- documentation update process
- roadmap update process

## Owner Review Process

Owner review confirms Phase 10.5 package review, expansion readiness, public trust, known limitations, public safety, stabilized operations handoff, controlled expansion gate, runbook, rollback readiness, final owner decision, and next Phase 10 step.

## Decision Rules

- Continue when no blockers remain and owner approval is recorded.
- Remain limited when local verification is blocked, owner conditions remain, or warnings still require watch.
- Pause expansion when public trust, support readiness, known limitations, or safety review is not ready.
- Rollback when critical safety, privacy, app availability, or owner rollback decisions appear.
- Expand only when build/typecheck/local verification passes, public trust and safety boundaries pass, operations handoff is ready, owner approval is recorded, and expansion conditions are documented.

## Expansion Blocker Rules

Expansion is blocked by unresolved Severity 1 issues, unapproved expansion-blocking Severity 2 issues, private data exposure, secret exposure, unsafe spiritual response pattern, missing public trust review, missing owner approval, missing rollback readiness, hidden known limitations, or any weakened safety boundary.

## Public Trust Blocker Rules

Public trust blocks expansion when Teoyube claims divine certainty, implies guaranteed prophecy, replaces professional support, hides known limitations, bypasses privacy/consent, hides confidence labels, removes explanation traces, removes Scripture anchors, weakens fallback states, hides service-disabled state, or creates hidden personalization.

## Safe Operations Handoff Rules

Operations handoff is blocked if release ownership, manual monitoring, feedback review, issue triage, known issue register, safe-fix process, rollback process, public trust refresh, weekly improvement loop, documentation updates, or roadmap updates are not defined.
